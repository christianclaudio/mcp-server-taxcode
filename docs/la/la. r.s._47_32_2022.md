# La. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 47:32 — Rates of Tax (Graduated Schedule through 2024)

- **Jurisdiction:** LA
- **Corpus:** La. R.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2022
- **Superseded Year:** 2025
- **Source:** [https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=101946](https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=101946)
- **Hierarchy:** `La. R.S. > Title 47 > Subtitle II > Chapter 1 > Part I > § 47:32`
- **Cross References:** `La. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 47:32`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated personal income tax rates of 1.85%, 3.50%, and 4.25% in Louisiana applicable through tax year 2024.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto sobre la renta personal graduadas de 1.85%, 3.50% y 4.25% en Luisiana aplicables hasta el año contributivo 2024.
