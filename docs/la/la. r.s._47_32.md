- **Jurisdiction:** LA
- **Corpus:** La. R.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated personal income tax rates of 1.85%, 3.50%, and 4.25% in Louisiana.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto sobre la renta personal graduadas de 1.85%, 3.50% y 4.25% en Luisiana.
