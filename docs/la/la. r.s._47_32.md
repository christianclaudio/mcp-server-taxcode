# La. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 47:32 — Flat Individual Income Tax Rate (Act 11 of 2024 3rd Ex. Sess.)

- **Jurisdiction:** LA
- **Corpus:** La. R.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2025
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=101946](https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=101946)
- **Hierarchy:** `La. R.S. > Title 47 > Subtitle II > Chapter 1 > Part I > § 47:32`
- **Cross References:** `La. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 47:32`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes a flat individual income tax rate of 3.00% on net taxable income effective for tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025 (Act 11 of 2024).

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone una tasa fija de impuesto sobre la renta del 3.00% sobre el ingreso neto gravable de individuos a partir del 1 de enero de 2025 (Act 11 de 2024).
