# RCW 82.87.040 — Tax Imposed on Long-Term Capital Gains

- **Jurisdiction:** WA
- **Corpus:** RCW
- **Effective Year:** 2022
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.87.040](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.87.040)
- **Hierarchy:** `RCW > Title 82 > Chapter 82.87 > Section 82.87.040`
- **Cross References:** `RCW 82.87.020`, `Wash. Const. art. VII § 1`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

7% excise tax on individual annual net long-term capital gains allocated to Washington in excess of the statutory standard deduction ($250,000 indexed for inflation: $262,000 for 2023, $270,000 for 2024 and $278,000 for 2025), plus, beginning January 1, 2025, an additional 2.9% on the portion of an individual's Washington capital gains exceeding $1,000,000.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impuesto especial del 7% sobre ganancias de capital a largo plazo de individuos que excedan la deducción estándar anual ($250,000 indexada por inflación: $262,000 para 2023, $270,000 para 2024 y $278,000 para 2025), más, a partir del 1 de enero de 2025, un impuesto adicional del 2.9% sobre la porción de las ganancias de capital de Washington de un individuo que exceda $1,000,000.
