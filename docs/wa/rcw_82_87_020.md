# RCW 82.87.020 — Capital Gains Tax Definitions and Resident Sourcing

- **Jurisdiction:** WA
- **Corpus:** RCW
- **Effective Year:** 2022
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.87.020](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.87.020)
- **Hierarchy:** `RCW > Title 82 > Chapter 82.87 > Section 82.87.020`
- **Cross References:** `RCW 82.87.040`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines resident for Washington capital gains excise tax purposes as an individual domiciled in Washington at the time the long-term capital asset was sold or exchanged.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define residente de Washington para el impuesto de ganancias de capital como persona natural domiciliada en el estado al momento de la venta o intercambio.
