# Mich. Comp. Laws § 206.51 — Tax Rate on Taxable Income of Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** MI
- **Corpus:** MCL
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes a flat individual income tax rate of 4.25% on taxable net income in Michigan.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone una tasa uniforme de impuesto sobre ingresos individuales del 4.25% sobre el ingreso neto gravable en Michigan.
