# O.C.G.A. § 48-7-1 — Income Tax Definitions / Residency Rules

- **Jurisdiction:** GA
- **Corpus:** OCGA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://dor.georgia.gov/](https://dor.georgia.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `O.C.G.A. > Title 48 > Chapter 7 > Article 1 > § 48-7-1`
- **Cross References:** `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-20`, `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-30`, `Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. r. 560-7-3-.01`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines Georgia resident as any individual who is a legal resident of Georgia on income tax day, who resides within the state for 183 days or more in the aggregate during the taxable year, or who maintains a permanent place of abode within the state.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define residente de Georgia como todo individuo con residencia legal en Georgia el día de pago del impuesto, que resida en el estado al menos 183 días del año contributivo, o que mantenga una vivienda permanente.
