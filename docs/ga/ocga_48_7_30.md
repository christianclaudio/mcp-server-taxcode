# O.C.G.A. § 48-7-30 — Taxation of Nonresidents

- **Jurisdiction:** GA
- **Corpus:** OCGA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://dor.georgia.gov/](https://dor.georgia.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `O.C.G.A. > Title 48 > Chapter 7 > Article 2 > § 48-7-30`
- **Cross References:** `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-1`, `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-20`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Taxes nonresidents on gross income derived from Georgia sources, including compensation for services performed within Georgia, property located in Georgia, and business conducted in the state.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Grava a personas no residentes sobre su ingreso bruto derivado de fuentes de Georgia, incluyendo compensación por servicios realizados en Georgia y actividades comerciales locales.
