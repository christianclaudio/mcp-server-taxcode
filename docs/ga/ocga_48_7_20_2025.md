# O.C.G.A. § 48-7-20 — Individual Income Tax Rates (2025 Flat Rate: 5.39%)

- **Jurisdiction:** GA
- **Corpus:** OCGA
- **Effective Year:** 2025
- **Superseded Year:** 2026
- **Source:** [https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/taxes-individuals](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/taxes-individuals)
- **Hierarchy:** `O.C.G.A. > Title 48 > Chapter 7 > Article 2 > § 48-7-20 (2025)`
- **Cross References:** `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-1`, `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-27`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Accelerates the reduction of the flat individual income tax rate to 5.39% for tax year 2025 pursuant to HB 1015.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Acelera la reducción de la tasa impositiva individual fija al 5.39% para el año contributivo 2025 bajo HB 1015.
