# O.C.G.A. § 48-7-20 — Individual Income Tax Rates (2026 Target Flat Rate: 4.99%)

- **Jurisdiction:** GA
- **Corpus:** OCGA
- **Effective Year:** 2026
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/individual-income-tax](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/individual-income-tax)
- **Hierarchy:** `O.C.G.A. > Title 48 > Chapter 7 > Article 2 > § 48-7-20 (2026)`
- **Cross References:** `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-1`, `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-27`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes the target flat individual income tax rate of 4.99% for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026 pursuant to HB 1023.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Establece la tasa impositiva fija objetivo de 4.99% sobre el ingreso neto tributable a partir del 1 de enero de 2026 bajo HB 1023.
