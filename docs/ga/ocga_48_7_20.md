# O.C.G.A. § 48-7-20 — Individual Income Tax Rates

- **Jurisdiction:** GA
- **Corpus:** OCGA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Source:** [https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/individual-income-tax](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/individual-income-tax)
- **Hierarchy:** `O.C.G.A. > Title 48 > Chapter 7 > Article 2 > § 48-7-20`
- **Cross References:** `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-1`, `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-27`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes a flat income tax rate on Georgia taxable net income of individuals (5.49% for 2024, 5.39% for 2025, transitioning towards 4.99% under HB 1023 / HB 1015).

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Establece una tasa impositiva fija (flat tax) sobre el ingreso neto tributable de individuos (5.49% para 2024, 5.39% para 2025, con reducciones graduales hacia 4.99% bajo HB 1023).
