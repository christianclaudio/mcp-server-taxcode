# O.C.G.A. § 48-7-27 — Computation of Georgia Taxable Net Income

- **Jurisdiction:** GA
- **Corpus:** OCGA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/taxes-individuals](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/taxes-individuals)
- **Hierarchy:** `O.C.G.A. > Title 48 > Chapter 7 > Article 2 > § 48-7-27`
- **Cross References:** `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-20`, `26 U.S.C. § 62`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Calculates Georgia taxable net income from Federal AGI, allowing standard deductions of $12,000 (single) or $24,000 (married filing jointly), and a retirement income exclusion of up to $65,000 for individuals age 65 or older ($35,000 for ages 62-64).

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Dispone el cálculo del ingreso neto gravable a partir del AGI federal, deducción estándar de $12,000 (solteros) o $24,000 (casados), y exclusión de hasta $65,000 en ingresos de jubilación para mayores de 65 años.
