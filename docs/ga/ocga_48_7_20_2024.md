# O.C.G.A. § 48-7-20 — Individual Income Tax Rates (2024 Flat Rate: 5.39%)

- **Jurisdiction:** GA
- **Corpus:** OCGA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** 2025
- **Source:** [https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/taxes-individuals](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/taxes-individuals)
- **Hierarchy:** `O.C.G.A. > Title 48 > Chapter 7 > Article 2 > § 48-7-20 (2024)`
- **Cross References:** `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-1`, `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-27`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes a flat individual income tax rate of 5.39% on Georgia taxable net income for tax year 2024 pursuant to HB 1015 (2024), which lowered the 5.49% rate previously set for 2024; graduated brackets no longer apply.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Establece una tasa impositiva fija (flat tax) de 5.39% sobre el ingreso neto tributable de individuos para el año contributivo 2024 bajo HB 1015 (2024), que redujo la tasa de 5.49% fijada previamente para 2024; ya no se aplican tramos graduados.
