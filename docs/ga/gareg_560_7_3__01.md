# Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. r. 560-7-3-.01 — Residence and Domicile

- **Jurisdiction:** GA
- **Corpus:** GAREG
- **Effective Year:** 2023
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://rules.sos.ga.gov/gac/560-7-3-.01](https://rules.sos.ga.gov/gac/560-7-3-.01)
- **Hierarchy:** `Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. > Chapter 560-7 > Subject 560-7-3 > Rule 560-7-3-.01`
- **Cross References:** `O.C.G.A. § 48-7-1`, `26 U.S.C. § 937`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Georgia Department of Revenue rule governing residence and legal domicile. Domicile in Georgia is presumed to continue until the taxpayer establishes abandonment and acquisition of a permanent domicile elsewhere.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Reglamento del Departamento de Ingresos de Georgia sobre domicilio y residencia. El domicilio en Georgia se presume continuo a menos que el contribuyente demuestre abandono definitivo y adquisición de nuevo domicilio.
