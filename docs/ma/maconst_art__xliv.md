# Mass. Const. amend. art. XLIV — Fair Share Amendment (4% Millionaires' Surtax)

- **Jurisdiction:** MA
- **Corpus:** MACONST
- **Effective Year:** 2023
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://malegislature.gov/Laws/Constitution](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/Constitution)
- **Hierarchy:** `Mass. Const. > Amendments > Article XLIV`
- **Cross References:** `M.G.L. c. 62 § 4`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Constitutional amendment imposing an additional 4% surtax on the portion of annual taxable income exceeding $1,000,000, creating a top rate of 9.0%.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Enmienda constitucional que impone una sobretasa adicional del 4% sobre la porción del ingreso imponible anual que exceda $1,000,000 (indexado por inflación).
