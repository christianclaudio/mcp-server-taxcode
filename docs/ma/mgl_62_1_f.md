# M.G.L. c. 62 § 1(f) — Definition of Resident (Domicile and 183-Day Rule)

- **Jurisdiction:** MA
- **Corpus:** MGL
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleIX/Chapter62/Section1](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleIX/Chapter62/Section1)
- **Hierarchy:** `M.G.L. > Part I > Title IX > Chapter 62 > Section 1(f)`
- **Cross References:** `M.G.L. c. 62 § 4`

## Unofficial Reference Summary (English)

Defines resident as any natural person domiciled in Massachusetts, or non-domiciliary maintaining a permanent place of abode and spending more than 183 days in the state.

## Resumen de referencia no oficial (Español)

Define residente como persona domiciliada en Massachusetts o no domiciliada que mantenga un lugar permanente de morada y pase más de 183 días en el estado.
