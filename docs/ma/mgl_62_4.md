# M.G.L. c. 62 § 4 — Personal Income Tax Rates

- **Jurisdiction:** MA
- **Corpus:** MGL
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleIX/Chapter62/Section4](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleIX/Chapter62/Section4)
- **Hierarchy:** `M.G.L. > Part I > Title IX > Chapter 62 > Section 4`
- **Cross References:** `Mass. Const. amend. art. XLIV`, `M.G.L. c. 62 § 1`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Standard 5.0% flat income tax rate on Part B taxable personal income (wages, trade, business, and interest income).

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Tasa básica de impuesto sobre el ingreso personal de Massachusetts del 5.0% sobre ingreso imponible de Parte B (salarios, negocios y pensiones).
