# Md. Code Ann., Tax-Gen. § 10-101 — Definitions - Resident Individual

- **Jurisdiction:** MD
- **Corpus:** Md. Code
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtg&section=10-101](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtg&section=10-101)
- **Hierarchy:** `Md. Code > Tax-General > Title 10 > Subtitle 1 > § 10-101`
- **Cross References:** `Md. Code Ann., Tax-Gen. § 10-105`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines a resident individual as one who is domiciled in Maryland on the last day of the taxable year, or who maintains a place of abode in Maryland for more than six months and is physically present for 183 days or more.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define individuo residente como quien tiene domicilio en Maryland el último día del año contributivo, o mantiene morada por más de 6 meses y está presente físicamente 183 días o más.
