# Ohio Rev. Code § 5747.24 — Bright-Line Presumption of Domicile - Contact Periods

- **Jurisdiction:** OH
- **Corpus:** R.C.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5747.24](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5747.24)
- **Hierarchy:** `R.C. > Title 57 > Chapter 5747 > § 5747.24`
- **Cross References:** `Ohio Rev. Code § 5747.02`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Establishes bright-line residency rules: an individual with <= 212 Ohio contact periods is presumed non-resident if filing Form IT-NRS affidavit by October 15; an individual with >= 213 contact periods is presumed an Ohio resident.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Establece regla objetiva de periodos de contacto: quien tenga <= 212 periodos de contacto se presume no residente si radica declaración jurada IT-NRS antes del 15 de octubre; >= 213 periodos presume residencia.
