# Ohio Rev. Code § 5747.02 — Taxation of Personal Income - Rates

- **Jurisdiction:** OH
- **Corpus:** R.C.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5747.02](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5747.02)
- **Hierarchy:** `R.C. > Title 57 > Chapter 5747 > § 5747.02`
- **Cross References:** `Ohio Rev. Code § 5747.24`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Levies Ohio personal income tax with phased rate consolidation: 0% up to $26,050; 2.75% from $26,051 to $100,000; and 3.50% on non-business taxable income exceeding $100,000 for tax year 2024+.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Grava el ingreso personal en Ohio con consolidación de tasas: 0% hasta $26,050; 2.75% de $26,051 a $100,000; y 3.50% sobre el ingreso no comercial que exceda $100,000 para el año 2024+.
