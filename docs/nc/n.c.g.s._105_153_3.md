# N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-153.3 — Definitions - Resident Individual

- **Jurisdiction:** NC
- **Corpus:** N.C.G.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_105/Section_105-153.3.html](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_105/Section_105-153.3.html)
- **Hierarchy:** `N.C.G.S. > Chapter 105 > Article 4 > Part 2 > § 105-153.3`
- **Cross References:** `N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-153.7`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines resident individual as one domiciled in North Carolina or who resides in state for more than 183 days during the taxable year creating a rebuttable presumption of residency.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define individuo residente como quien reside o tiene domicilio en Carolina del Norte, o quien pasa más de 183 días en el estado durante el año contributivo presumiendo residencia.
