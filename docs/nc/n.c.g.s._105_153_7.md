# N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-153.7 — Individual Income Tax Imposition and Rate

- **Jurisdiction:** NC
- **Corpus:** N.C.G.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_105/Section_105-153.7.html](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_105/Section_105-153.7.html)
- **Hierarchy:** `N.C.G.S. > Chapter 105 > Article 4 > Part 2 > § 105-153.7`
- **Cross References:** `N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-153.3`, `N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-153.5`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes individual income tax at a decreasing flat rate: 4.5% for 2024, 4.25% for 2025, and 3.99% for taxable years beginning on or after 2026.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone un impuesto sobre la renta individual de tasa fija decreciente: 4.5% en 2024, 4.25% en 2025, y 3.99% para años tributarios 2026 y posteriores.
