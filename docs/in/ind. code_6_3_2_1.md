# Ind. Code § 6-3-2-1 — Imposition of Adjusted Gross Income Tax

- **Jurisdiction:** IN
- **Corpus:** Ind. Code
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/current/ic/title/6/article/3/chapter/2/](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/current/ic/title/6/article/3/chapter/2/)
- **Hierarchy:** `Ind. Code > Title 6 > Article 3 > Chapter 2 > § 6-3-2-1`
- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes flat adjusted gross income tax of 3.05% for 2024 alongside county local income taxes (LIT) in Indiana.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone una tasa fija de impuesto sobre el ingreso bruto ajustado del 3.05% para 2024 junto a impuestos locales de condado (LIT) en Indiana.
