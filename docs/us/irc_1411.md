# 26 U.S.C. § 1411 — Imposition of tax on net investment income

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** IRC
- **Effective Year:** 2010
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1411](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1411)
- **Hierarchy:** `Title 26 > Subtitle A > Chapter 2A > § 1411`
- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 933`, `26 CFR § 1.1411-1`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes a 3.8 percent tax on net investment income of individuals, estates, and trusts having modified adjusted gross income exceeding statutory thresholds ($200,000 single, $250,000 married filing jointly). Excluded Puerto Rico-source income under § 933 is not included in MAGI or net investment income.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone un impuesto de 3.8 por ciento sobre el ingreso neto de inversión para individuos con ingreso bruto ajustado modificado superior a los límites estatutarios ($200,000 solteros, $250,000 casados). El ingreso excluido bajo § 933 no forma parte del ingreso neto de inversión tributable.
