# 26 U.S.C. § 957 — Controlled foreign corporations; United States persons

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** IRC
- **Effective Year:** 1962
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section957](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section957)
- **Hierarchy:** `Title 26 > Subtitle A > Chapter 1 > Subchapter N > Part III > Subpart F > § 957`
- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 951`, `26 U.S.C. § 933`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines controlled foreign corporations (CFCs). Under subsection (c), bona fide residents of Puerto Rico are excluded from the definition of a United States person with respect to corporations organized under the laws of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, exempting them from Subpart F and GILTI reporting.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define corporaciones foráneas controladas (CFC). Bajo el inciso (c), los residentes de buena fe de Puerto Rico quedan excluidos de la definición de persona de EE.UU. respecto a corporaciones creadas bajo las leyes del Estado Libre Asociado de Puerto Rico, eximiéndoles de Subpart F y GILTI.
