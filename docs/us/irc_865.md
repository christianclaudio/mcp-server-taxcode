# 26 U.S.C. § 865 — Source rules for personal property sales

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** IRC
- **Effective Year:** 1986
- **Source:** [https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section865](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section865)
- **Hierarchy:** `Title 26 > Subtitle A > Chapter 1 > Subchapter N > Part I > § 865`
- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 937`, `26 CFR § 1.937-2`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Except as otherwise provided in this section, income from the sale of personal property (including corporate stock, debt securities, and marketable assets) by a United States resident shall be sourced in the United States, and by a non-resident shall be sourced outside the United States.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Salvo disposición expresa en contrario, el ingreso derivado de la venta de bienes personales (incluyendo acciones y valores negociables) se atribuirá a la residencia del vendedor.
