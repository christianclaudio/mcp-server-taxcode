# 26 CFR § 1.937-3 — Income effectively connected with the conduct of a trade or business in a possession

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** CFR
- **Effective Year:** 2006
- **Source:** [https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.937-3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.937-3)
- **Hierarchy:** `Title 26 > Chapter I > Subchapter A > Part 1 > § 1.937-3`
- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 937`, `26 CFR § 1.937-2`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Principles for determining whether income is effectively connected with the active conduct of a trade or business within a possession. Evaluates asset-use test and business-activities test for both possession-source and foreign-source income.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Principios para determinar si el ingreso está efectivamente conectado con la conducción activa de una industria o negocio en una posesión, aplicando pruebas de uso de activos y actividades operacionales.
