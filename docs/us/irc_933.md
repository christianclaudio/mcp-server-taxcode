# 26 U.S.C. § 933 — Income from sources within Puerto Rico

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** IRC
- **Effective Year:** 1954
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force

- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 937`, `26 CFR § 1.937-1`, `26 U.S.C. § 865`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

In the case of an individual who is a bona fide resident of Puerto Rico during the entire taxable year, gross income does not include income derived from sources within Puerto Rico (except amounts received for services performed as an employee of the United States or any agency thereof); but such individual shall not be allowed as a deduction from his gross income any deductions (other than the deduction under section 151, relating to personal exemptions) or any credit properly allocable to or chargeable against amounts excluded from gross income.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

En el caso de un individuo que sea residente de buena fe de Puerto Rico durante todo el año tributario, el ingreso bruto no incluirá ingresos derivados de fuentes dentro de Puerto Rico (excepto cantidades recibidas por servicios prestados como empleado de los Estados Unidos o cualquiera de sus agencias); no permitiéndose deducciones atribuibles a dicho ingreso.
