# 26 CFR § 1.937-2 — Income from sources within a possession

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** CFR
- **Effective Year:** 2006
- **Source:** [https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.937-2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.937-2)
- **Hierarchy:** `Title 26 > Chapter I > Subchapter A > Part 1 > § 1.937-2`
- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 937`, `26 CFR § 1.937-1`, `26 U.S.C. § 865`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Sourcing rules for possessions and 10-year built-in gain lookback rules under Treas. Reg. § 1.937-2(f) for property held prior to establishing bona fide residency. Allows election under § 1.937-2(f)(1)(vi) to allocate gain based on FMV on residency date (marketable securities) or linear time apportionment.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Reglas de fuente para posesiones y regla de lookback de 10 años para ganancias acumuladas (built-in gains) sobre bienes adquiridos antes de establecer residencia bajo Treas. Reg. § 1.937-2(f), permitiendo elección para segregar valor acumulado previo y posterior.
