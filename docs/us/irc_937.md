# 26 U.S.C. § 937 — Residence and source rules involving possessions

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** IRC
- **Effective Year:** 2004
- **Source:** [https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section937](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section937)
- **Hierarchy:** `Title 26 > Subtitle A > Chapter 1 > Subchapter N > Part III > Subpart D > § 937`
- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 933`, `26 CFR § 1.937-1`, `26 CFR § 1.937-2`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Rules for determining bona fide residency in possessions: (a) An individual is a bona fide resident if present in the possession for at least 183 days during the taxable year, does not have a tax home outside the possession, and does not have a closer connection to the United States or a foreign country. (b) Source rules: Principles for determining whether income is derived from sources within a possession or effectively connected with the conduct of a trade or business within a possession.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Establece los requisitos estatutarios para determinar residencia de buena fe en posesiones (presencia física de 183 días, centro de actividad tributaria 'tax home' y conexión más cercana), así como las reglas de fuente de ingresos aplicables a posesiones y territorios.
