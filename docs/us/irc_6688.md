# 26 U.S.C. § 6688 — Assessable penalties with respect to information required to be furnished under section 7654 / Penalidades tasables respecto a información que debe suministrarse bajo la sección 7654

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** IRC
- **Effective Year:** 1972
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://uscode.house.gov/download/releasepoints/us/pl/119/121/xml_usc26@119-121.zip](https://uscode.house.gov/download/releasepoints/us/pl/119/121/xml_usc26@119-121.zip)
- **Hierarchy:** `Title 26 > Subtitle F > Chapter 68 > Subchapter B > Part I > § 6688 (OLRC release point 119-121)`
- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 937`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

In addition to any criminal penalty provided by law, any person described in section 7654(a) who is required under section 937(c) or by regulations prescribed under section 7654 to furnish information and who fails to comply with such requirement at the time prescribed by such regulations unless it is shown that such failure is due to reasonable cause and not to willful neglect, shall pay (upon notice and demand by the Secretary and in the same manner as tax) a penalty of $1,000 for each such failure.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Resumen en español; el texto oficial está en inglés. Además de cualquier penalidad criminal, toda persona descrita en la sección 7654(a) que deba suministrar información bajo la sección 937(c) o bajo los reglamentos de la sección 7654 y no cumpla a tiempo, salvo que demuestre causa razonable y no negligencia voluntaria, pagará una penalidad de $1,000 por cada incumplimiento.
