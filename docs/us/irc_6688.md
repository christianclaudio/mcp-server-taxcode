# 26 U.S.C. § 6688 — Assessable penalties with respect to information required under section 937

- **Jurisdiction:** US
- **Corpus:** IRC
- **Effective Year:** 2004
- **Source:** [https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6688](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6688)
- **Hierarchy:** `Title 26 > Subtitle F > Chapter 68 > Subchapter B > § 6688`
- **Cross References:** `26 U.S.C. § 937`, `IRS Form 8898`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes a statutory penalty of $1,000 for failure to file information required under section 937(c) (IRS Form 8898 for individuals establishing or terminating bona fide residence in a possession) without reasonable cause.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone una penalidad estatutaria de $1,000 por no presentar la declaración informativa requerida bajo la sección 937(c) (Formulario IRS 8898 al establecer o culminar residencia en una posesión) sin causa justificada.
