# Ala. Code § 40-18-2 — Tax on Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** AL
- **Corpus:** Ala. Code
- **Effective Year:** 2024

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates of 2%, 4%, and 5% on taxable net income of individuals in Alabama.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas impositivas individuales graduadas del 2%, 4% y 5% sobre el ingreso neto gravable de individuos en Alabama.
