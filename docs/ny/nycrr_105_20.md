# 20 NYCRR § 105.20 — Rules for Determining Domicile (5 Primary Factors)

- **Jurisdiction:** NY
- **Corpus:** NYCRR
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.tax.ny.gov/](https://www.tax.ny.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `20 NYCRR > Chapter II > Subchapter A > Part 105 > § 105.20`
- **Cross References:** `NY Tax Law § 605`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

New York DTF regulation establishing the 5 primary factors for domicile audit evaluation: Home, Active Business Involvement, Time Allocation, Items of Personal Significance ('Near and Dear'), and Family.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Reglamento de auditoría de domicilio de NY que establece los 5 factores primarios: Vivienda, Negocio Activo, Tiempo, Objetos de Valor Sentimental ('Near and Dear') y Familia.
