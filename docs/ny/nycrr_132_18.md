# 20 NYCRR § 132.18 — Convenience of the Employer Rule

- **Jurisdiction:** NY
- **Corpus:** NYCRR
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.tax.ny.gov/](https://www.tax.ny.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `20 NYCRR > Chapter II > Subchapter A > Part 132 > § 132.18`
- **Cross References:** `NY Tax Law § 605`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Provides that compensation of a nonresident employee for services performed out of state is sourced to New York if the services are performed outside NY for the employee's convenience rather than by necessity of the employer.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Dispone que la compensación de empleados no residentes por servicios realizados fuera del estado se atribuye a Nueva York si el trabajo remoto se realiza por conveniencia del empleado y no por necesidad del empleador.
