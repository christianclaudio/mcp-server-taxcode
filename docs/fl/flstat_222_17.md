# Fla. Stat. § 222.17 — Manifesting and Evidencing Domicile in Florida

- **Jurisdiction:** FL
- **Corpus:** FLSTAT
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.flsenate.gov/](https://www.flsenate.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `Fla. Stat. > Title XV > Chapter 222 > § 222.17`
- **Cross References:** `Fla. Const. Art. VII § 5`, `Fla. Stat. § 196.031`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Authorizes any person who has established a domicile in Florida to manifest that domicile by filing a sworn, notarized statement of domicile with the clerk of the circuit court in the county of residence.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Autoriza a toda persona que haya establecido domicilio en Florida a manifestarlo formalmente mediante declaración jurada radicada ante el secretario del tribunal de circuito del condado correspondiente.
