# Fla. Stat. § 196.031 — Exemption of Homesteads

- **Jurisdiction:** FL
- **Corpus:** FLSTAT
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.flsenate.gov/](https://www.flsenate.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `Fla. Stat. > Title XIV > Chapter 196 > § 196.031`
- **Cross References:** `Fla. Stat. § 222.17`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Grants up to $50,000 homestead property tax exemption to persons who maintain permanent residence. Claiming Florida homestead establishes prima facie proof of Florida domicile.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Concede hasta $50,000 de exención contributiva sobre la propiedad principal a residentes permanentes. Solicitar la exención de 'homestead' constituye prueba prima facie de domicilio en Florida.
