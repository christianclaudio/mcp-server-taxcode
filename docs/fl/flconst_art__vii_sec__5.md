# Fla. Const. Art. VII § 5 — Constitutional Prohibition of Personal Income Tax

- **Jurisdiction:** FL
- **Corpus:** FLCONST
- **Effective Year:** 1968
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.flsenate.gov/](https://www.flsenate.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `Fla. Const. > Article VII > Section 5`
- **Cross References:** `Fla. Stat. § 222.17`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Prohibits any tax upon the income of natural persons who are residents or citizens of Florida, guaranteeing the absence of state individual income taxation.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Prohíbe constitucionalmente cualquier impuesto sobre el ingreso de personas naturales residentes o ciudadanas de Florida, garantizando la ausencia de impuesto estatal sobre ingresos individuales.
