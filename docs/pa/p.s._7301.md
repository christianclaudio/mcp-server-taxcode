# 72 P.S. § 7301 — Definitions - Resident Individual

- **Jurisdiction:** PA
- **Corpus:** P.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=72&div=0&chpt=73](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=72&div=0&chpt=73)
- **Hierarchy:** `72 P.S. > Chapter 73 > § 7301`
- **Cross References:** `72 P.S. § 7302`, `61 Pa. Code § 101.1`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines a resident individual as one domiciled in Pennsylvania or who maintains a permanent place of abode and spends in the aggregate more than 183 days in the commonwealth.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define individuo residente como quien tiene domicilio en Pensilvania o mantiene un lugar permanente de morada y pasa más de 183 días en el estado.
