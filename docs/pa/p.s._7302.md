# 72 P.S. § 7302 — Imposition of Tax

- **Jurisdiction:** PA
- **Corpus:** P.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=72&div=0&chpt=73](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=72&div=0&chpt=73)
- **Hierarchy:** `72 P.S. > Chapter 73 > § 7302`
- **Cross References:** `72 P.S. § 7301`, `72 P.S. § 7303`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes Pennsylvania personal income tax at a constitutional flat rate of 3.07% on eight specified classes of taxable income.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone un impuesto sobre la renta personal con tasa uniforme y fija del 3.07% sobre ocho clases especificadas de ingresos gravables.
