# Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17041 — Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets

- **Jurisdiction:** CA
- **Corpus:** RTC
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ftb.ca.gov/](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code > Part 10 > Chapter 2 > § 17041`
- **Cross References:** `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17014`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated income tax rates on California taxable income ranging from 1% to 12.3%, plus a 1% Mental Health Services Tax surcharge on taxable income exceeding $1,000,000, creating a top marginal individual rate of 13.3%.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Grava el ingreso tributable de California con tasas escalonadas del 1% al 12.3%, más 1% de recargo para servicios de salud mental sobre ingresos mayores a $1M (Proposición 63), para una tasa marginal máxima de 13.3%.
