## Unofficial Reference Summary (English)

California taxable income uses federal income as a starting point with state adjustments and permitted deductions. Taxpayers compare the California standard deduction with allowable California itemized deductions, subject to state limits; federal taxable income cannot simply be copied.

## Resumen de referencia no oficial (Español)

El ingreso tributable de California parte del ingreso federal con ajustes y deducciones estatales. Se compara la deducción estándar de California con deducciones detalladas permitidas y sus límites; no basta copiar el ingreso tributable federal.
