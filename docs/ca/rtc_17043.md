# Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17043 — Behavioral Health Services Tax: additional 1%

- **Jurisdiction:** CA
- **Corpus:** RTC
- **Effective Year:** 2025
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-booklet.pdf](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-booklet.pdf) — Unofficial bilingual reference summary, not statutory text. Any hash covers the cited source file, not this summary. 2025 Form 540 instructions, line 62, confirm the new name; the 2024 booklet confirms the former name.
- **Hierarchy:** `CA > Individual income tax > Behavioral Health Services Tax: additional 1% > 17043`
- **Cross References:** `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17041`

## Unofficial Reference Summary (English)

An additional 1% tax applies only to California taxable income exceeding $1,000,000, including on a joint return with the same threshold. From January 1, 2025 it is named Behavioral Health Services Tax, replacing Mental Health Services Tax. Crossing the threshold does not impose 1% on all income.

## Resumen de referencia no oficial (Español)

Un impuesto adicional del 1% grava solo el ingreso tributable de California que exceda $1,000,000, con igual umbral en declaración conjunta. Desde el 1 de enero de 2025 se denomina Behavioral Health Services Tax, en sustitución de Mental Health Services Tax. Superar el umbral no grava todo el ingreso al 1%.
