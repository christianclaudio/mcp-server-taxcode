# 18 CCR § 17014 — FTB Residence and Domicile Guidelines

- **Jurisdiction:** CA
- **Corpus:** CACCR
- **Effective Year:** 2023
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ftb.ca.gov/](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `18 CCR > Division 3 > Chapter 2.5 > Subchapter 1 > § 17014`
- **Cross References:** `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17014`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

FTB regulation detailing domicile and residence determination. A person may have multiple residences but only one domicile. Applies the closest connection test evaluating real property, family, bank accounts, business affiliations, and licenses.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Reglamento de la FTB sobre residencia y domicilio. Aplica el criterio de conexión más cercana evaluando propiedades inmuebles, familia, cuentas bancarias, licencias profesionales y afiliaciones comerciales.
