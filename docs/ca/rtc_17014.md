# Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17014 — Definition of Resident (Non-Temporary Purpose Test)

- **Jurisdiction:** CA
- **Corpus:** RTC
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ftb.ca.gov/](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code > Part 10 > Chapter 1 > § 17014`
- **Cross References:** `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17041`, `18 CCR § 17014`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines resident as every individual who is in California for other than a temporary or transitory purpose, and every individual domiciled in California who is outside the state for a temporary or transitory purpose. Presumption under § 17016 at 9 months.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define residente como todo individuo que permanezca en California para fines que no sean temporales o transitorios, y todo domiciliado que esté fuera del estado de forma temporal. Aplica el criterio de conexión más cercana.
