# Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17951 — Gross Income of Nonresidents and Sourcing

- **Jurisdiction:** CA
- **Corpus:** RTC
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ftb.ca.gov/](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code > Part 10 > Chapter 9 > § 17951`
- **Cross References:** `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17014`, `Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 17041`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

In the case of nonresidents, gross income includes only gross income from sources within California, including income from a business, trade, or profession conducted in the state, and compensation for personal services performed in California.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Para no residentes, el ingreso bruto comprende únicamente ingresos derivados de fuentes en California, incluyendo negocios, profesiones y servicios personales realizados dentro del estado.
