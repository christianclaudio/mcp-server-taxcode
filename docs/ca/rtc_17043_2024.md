## Unofficial Reference Summary (English)

For 2024 the additional 1% Mental Health Services Tax applies to California taxable income above $1,000,000. It taxes the excess only and the threshold is not doubled for joint filers. This dated source establishes the 2024 name; unverified earlier years are not reconstructed.

Para 2024 el Mental Health Services Tax adicional del 1% grava el ingreso tributable de California superior a $1,000,000. Solo grava el exceso y el umbral no se duplica en declaración conjunta. La fuente fechada confirma el nombre de 2024; no se reconstruyen años anteriores sin verificar.
