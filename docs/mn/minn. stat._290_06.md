# Minn. Stat. § 290.06 — Rates of Tax - Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** MN
- **Corpus:** Minn. Stat.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates: 5.35%, 6.80%, 7.85%, and top rate 9.85% in Minnesota.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas impositivas individuales graduadas: 5.35%, 6.80%, 7.85% y tasa máxima de 9.85% en Minnesota.
