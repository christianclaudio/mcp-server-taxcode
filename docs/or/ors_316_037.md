# Or. Rev. Stat. § 316.037 — Imposition and Rate of Personal Income Tax

- **Jurisdiction:** OR
- **Corpus:** ORS
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors316.html](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors316.html)

- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates of 4.75%, 6.75%, 8.75%, and top rate 9.90% in Oregon.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto sobre la renta individual graduadas de 4.75%, 6.75%, 8.75% y tasa máxima de 9.90% en Oregón.
