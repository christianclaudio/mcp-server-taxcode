# Tenn. Const. art. II, § 28 — Prohibition of Tax on Personal Income

- **Jurisdiction:** TN
- **Corpus:** Tenn. Const.
- **Effective Year:** 2014
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Constitutional amendment prohibiting any state or local tax upon payroll or earned personal income in Tennessee.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Enmienda constitucional que prohíbe cualquier impuesto estatal o local sobre nómina o ingresos individuales del trabajo en Tennessee.
