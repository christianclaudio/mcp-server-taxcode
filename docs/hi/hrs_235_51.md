# Haw. Rev. Stat. § 235-51 — Tax on Individuals, Estates, and Trusts

- **Jurisdiction:** HI
- **Corpus:** HRS
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/docs/hrs.htm](https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/docs/hrs.htm)
- **Hierarchy:** `HRS > Title 14 > Chapter 235 > Part I > § 235-51`
- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates up to 11.0% under Act 97 (2024 Hawaii tax reform).

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto sobre la renta individual graduadas de hasta 11.0% bajo la Ley 97 (reforma contributiva de Hawaii de 2024).
