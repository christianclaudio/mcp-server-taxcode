# A.R.S. § 43-104 — Definitions - Resident and Presumption of Residency

- **Jurisdiction:** AZ
- **Corpus:** A.R.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.azleg.gov/ars/43/00104.htm](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/43/00104.htm)
- **Hierarchy:** `A.R.S. > Title 43 > Chapter 1 > Article 1 > § 43-104`
- **Cross References:** `A.R.S. § 43-1011`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines resident as every individual in Arizona for other than a temporary or transitory purpose; individuals spending in the aggregate more than nine months (270 days) of the tax year in state are presumed residents.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define residente como todo individuo en Arizona para fines no temporales ni transitorios; quien pase en agregado más de nueve meses (270 días) en el estado se presume residente.
