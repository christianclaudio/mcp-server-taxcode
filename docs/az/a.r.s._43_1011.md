# A.R.S. § 43-1011 — Taxes and Tax Rates - Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** AZ
- **Corpus:** A.R.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.azleg.gov/ars/43/01011.htm](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/43/01011.htm)
- **Hierarchy:** `A.R.S. > Title 43 > Chapter 10 > Article 1 > § 43-1011`
- **Cross References:** `A.R.S. § 43-104`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes a flat individual income tax rate of 2.50% on Arizona taxable income, enacted by SB 1828 to replace graduated individual tax brackets.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone una tasa impositiva fija del 2.50% sobre el ingreso gravable de Arizona de individuos, promulgada bajo SB 1828 eliminando tramos graduales anteriores.
