# 30 Del. C. § 1102 — Levy and Rates of Personal Income Tax

- **Jurisdiction:** DE
- **Corpus:** Del. Code
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force

- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated income tax rates from 2.2% to 6.6% and applies convenience of employer rules to nonresident compensation.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto sobre la renta graduadas del 2.2% al 6.6% y aplica reglas de conveniencia del empleador a remuneraciones de no residentes.
