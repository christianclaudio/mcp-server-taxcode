# N.J.S.A. 54A:1-2 — Definition of Resident Individual

- **Jurisdiction:** NJ
- **Corpus:** NJSA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://lis.njleg.state.nj.us/](https://lis.njleg.state.nj.us/)
- **Hierarchy:** `N.J.S.A. > Title 54A > Chapter 1 > Section 54A:1-2`
- **Cross References:** `N.J.S.A. 54A:2-1`, `N.J.A.C. 18:35-5.2`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines resident individual based on domicile or statutory residency (maintaining a permanent place of abode in NJ and spending more than 183 days in NJ).

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define residente de NJ por domicilio (salvo morada permanente exterior con <= 30 días en NJ) o residente estatutario con morada permanente en NJ y más de 183 días.
