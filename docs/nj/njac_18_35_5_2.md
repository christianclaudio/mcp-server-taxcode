# N.J.A.C. 18:35-5.2 — Convenience of the Employer Rule (Reciprocal Sourcing)

- **Jurisdiction:** NJ
- **Corpus:** NJAC
- **Effective Year:** 2023
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/)
- **Hierarchy:** `N.J.A.C. > Title 18 > Chapter 35 > Subchapter 5 > Section 18:35-5.2`
- **Cross References:** `N.J.S.A. 54A:5-8`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Reciprocal convenience of employer sourcing rule enacted in 2023 sourcing telecommuting compensation of nonresidents to NJ if their resident state imposes a convenience rule.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Regla recíproca de conveniencia del patrono aprobada en 2023 que grava a no residentes que teletrabajen si su estado de residencia aplica una regla similar (ej. Nueva York).
