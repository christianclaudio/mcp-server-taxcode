# N.J.S.A. 54A:2-1 — New Jersey Gross Income Tax Rates

- **Jurisdiction:** NJ
- **Corpus:** NJSA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://lis.njleg.state.nj.us/](https://lis.njleg.state.nj.us/)
- **Hierarchy:** `N.J.S.A. > Title 54A > Chapter 2 > Section 54A:2-1`
- **Cross References:** `N.J.S.A. 54A:1-2`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Graduated gross income tax brackets ranging from 1.4% up to top marginal rate of 10.75% on taxable income exceeding $1,000,000.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Escalas progresivas de impuesto sobre el ingreso bruto que varían entre 1.4% y una tasa marginal máxima de 10.75% para ingresos mayores a $1,000,000.
