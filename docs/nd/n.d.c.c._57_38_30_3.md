# N.D. Cent. Code § 57-38-30.3 — Simplified Method for Computing Individual Income Tax

- **Jurisdiction:** ND
- **Corpus:** N.D.C.C.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ndlegis.gov/cencode/t57c38.pdf](https://www.ndlegis.gov/cencode/t57c38.pdf)
- **Hierarchy:** `N.D.C.C. > Title 57 > Chapter 57-38 > § 57-38-30.3`
- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes individual income tax at 0% up to $44,725; 1.95% to $225,975; and top rate 2.50% (HB 1158) in North Dakota.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone impuesto sobre la renta individual de 0% hasta $44,725; 1.95% a $225,975; y tasa máxima de 2.50% (HB 1158) en Dakota del Norte.
