# W. Va. Code § 11-21-4e — Rate of Tax - Taxable Years Beginning After 2022

- **Jurisdiction:** WV
- **Corpus:** W. Va. Code
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [http://www.wvlegislature.gov/WVCODE/Code.cfm?chap=11&art=21#21-4e](http://www.wvlegislature.gov/WVCODE/Code.cfm?chap=11&art=21#21-4e)
- **Hierarchy:** `W. Va. Code > Chapter 11 > Article 21 > Part I > § 11-21-4e`
- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates from 2.36% to top rate 5.12% enacted under HB 2526 in West Virginia.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto sobre ingresos individuales graduadas del 2.36% a tasa máxima de 5.12% bajo HB 2526 en West Virginia.
