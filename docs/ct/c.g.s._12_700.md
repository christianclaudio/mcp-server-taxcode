# Conn. Gen. Stat. § 12-700 — Imposition of Income Tax and Rates

- **Jurisdiction:** CT
- **Corpus:** C.G.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_229.htm#sec_12-700](https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_229.htm#sec_12-700)
- **Hierarchy:** `C.G.S. > Title 12 > Chapter 229 > § 12-700`
- **Cross References:** `Conn. Gen. Stat. § 12-701`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated personal income tax rates from 3.0% to 6.99% on resident Connecticut taxable income with benefit phase-out recaptures for high earners.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas impositivas graduadas del 3.0% al 6.99% sobre el ingreso neto de residentes, con eliminación progresiva de tramos de exención en ingresos altos.
