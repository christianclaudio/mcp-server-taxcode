# Conn. Gen. Stat. § 12-701 — Definitions - Resident and Sourcing

- **Jurisdiction:** CT
- **Corpus:** C.G.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_229.htm#sec_12-701](https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_229.htm#sec_12-701)
- **Hierarchy:** `C.G.S. > Title 12 > Chapter 229 > § 12-701`
- **Cross References:** `Conn. Gen. Stat. § 12-700`, `Conn. Gen. Stat. § 12-711`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines resident as an individual domiciled in Connecticut or who maintains a permanent place of abode and spends in the aggregate more than 183 days of the taxable year in state.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define residente statutario como quien mantiene un lugar permanente de morada en Connecticut y pasa en total más de 183 días en el año contributivo.
