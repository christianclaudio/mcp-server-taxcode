# Conn. Gen. Stat. § 12-711 — Convenience of Employer Rule

- **Jurisdiction:** CT
- **Corpus:** C.G.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_229.htm#sec_12-711](https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_229.htm#sec_12-711)
- **Hierarchy:** `C.G.S. > Title 12 > Chapter 229 > § 12-711`
- **Cross References:** `Conn. Gen. Stat. § 12-701`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Applies a reciprocal convenience of the employer test to nonresident compensation earned while telecommuting for a Connecticut employer if employee's state applies a convenience rule.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Aplica la regla de conveniencia del patrono para determinar la fuente de ingresos salariales de no residentes que teletrabajan si su estado de residencia aplica una regla similar.
