# Colo. Rev. Stat. § 39-22-104 — Levy of Tax - Single Rate for Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** CO
- **Corpus:** C.R.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://leg.colorado.gov/agencies/office-legislative-legal-services/colorado-revised-statutes](https://leg.colorado.gov/agencies/office-legislative-legal-services/colorado-revised-statutes)
- **Hierarchy:** `C.R.S. > Title 39 > Article 22 > Part 1 > § 39-22-104`
- **Cross References:** `Colo. Rev. Stat. § 39-22-103`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes a flat tax of 4.40% on Colorado modified federal taxable income of individuals pursuant to voter-approved Proposition 121 (reduced from 4.55%).

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone una tasa uniforme del 4.40% sobre el ingreso federal tributable modificado de individuos de Colorado bajo la Proposición 121 aprobada por los electores.
