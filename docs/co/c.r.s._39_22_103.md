# Colo. Rev. Stat. § 39-22-103 — Definitions - Resident Individual

- **Jurisdiction:** CO
- **Corpus:** C.R.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://leg.colorado.gov/agencies/office-legislative-legal-services/colorado-revised-statutes](https://leg.colorado.gov/agencies/office-legislative-legal-services/colorado-revised-statutes)
- **Hierarchy:** `C.R.S. > Title 39 > Article 22 > Part 1 > § 39-22-103`
- **Cross References:** `Colo. Rev. Stat. § 39-22-104`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines a resident individual as one domiciled in Colorado, or who maintains a permanent place of abode in Colorado and spends in the aggregate more than six months of the taxable year in Colorado.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define individuo residente como quien tiene domicilio en Colorado, o mantiene morada permanente y pasa en agregado más de seis meses del año contributivo en Colorado.
