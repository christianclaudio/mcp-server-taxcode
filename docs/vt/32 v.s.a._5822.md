- **Jurisdiction:** VT
- **Corpus:** 32 V.S.A.

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates: 3.35%, 6.60%, 7.60%, and top rate 8.75% in Vermont.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto individual sobre la renta: 3.35%, 6.60%, 7.60% y tasa máxima de 8.75% en Vermont.
