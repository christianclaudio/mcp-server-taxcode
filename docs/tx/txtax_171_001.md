# Tex. Tax Code § 171.001 — Texas Franchise Tax (Entity Level)

- **Jurisdiction:** TX
- **Corpus:** TXTAX
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/)
- **Hierarchy:** `Tex. Tax Code > Title 2 > Subtitle F > Chapter 171 > § 171.001`
- **Cross References:** `Tex. Const. art. VIII § 24-a`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes franchise tax on each taxable entity chartered or organized in Texas or doing business in Texas based on gross margin, maintaining zero taxation on individuals.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone el impuesto de franquicia únicamente a entidades comerciales organizadas o con negocios en Texas sobre su margen bruto, sin gravar a personas naturales.
