# 35 ILCS 5/201 — Illinois Individual Income Tax Rate

- **Jurisdiction:** IL
- **Corpus:** ILCS
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=577](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=577)
- **Hierarchy:** `35 ILCS 5/ > Article 2 > Section 201`
- **Cross References:** `35 ILCS 5/1501(a)(20)`, `35 ILCS 5/301`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Constitutionally mandated non-graduated flat income tax rate of 4.95% on net income of individuals, trusts, and estates.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impuesto estatal no graduado a tasa fija del 4.95% sobre el ingreso neto de personas naturales, mandatado por la Constitución de Illinois.
