## Unofficial Reference Summary (English)

Individual base income starts with federal adjusted gross income and Illinois modifications. Qualifying federally included retirement income, including eligible pension, IRA and Social Security income, may be subtracted. Not every payment described as retirement qualifies automatically.

## Resumen de referencia no oficial (Español)

El ingreso base individual parte del ingreso bruto ajustado federal y ajustes de Illinois. Puede restarse ingreso de retiro elegible incluido federalmente, como ciertas pensiones, IRA y Seguro Social. No todo pago denominado de retiro califica automáticamente.
