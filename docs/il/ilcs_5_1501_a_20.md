# 35 ILCS 5/1501(a)(20) — Definition of Resident

- **Jurisdiction:** IL
- **Corpus:** ILCS
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=577](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=577)
- **Hierarchy:** `35 ILCS 5/ > Article 15 > Section 1501(a)(20)`
- **Cross References:** `35 ILCS 5/201`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Defines resident as an individual who is in Illinois for other than a temporary or transitory purpose during the taxable year or who is domiciled in Illinois.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Define residente como persona en Illinois para fines no transitorios o temporales, o domiciliada en Illinois ausente temporalmente.
