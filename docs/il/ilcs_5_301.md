# 35 ILCS 5/301 — Compensation and Nonresident Income Sourcing

- **Jurisdiction:** IL
- **Corpus:** ILCS
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=577](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=577)
- **Hierarchy:** `35 ILCS 5/ > Article 3 > Section 301`
- **Cross References:** `35 ILCS 5/201`

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Rules for allocating and sourcing compensation and business income of nonresidents based on physical days of services performed within Illinois.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Reglas de asignación de ingresos y salarios de no residentes a Illinois basándose en servicios efectivamente prestados en el estado.
