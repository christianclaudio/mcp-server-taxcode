# N.H. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 77:1 — Rate of Tax on Interest and Dividends

- **Jurisdiction:** NH
- **Corpus:** RSA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/v/77/77-1.htm](https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/v/77/77-1.htm)

- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Levies tax strictly on dividend and interest income (phasing out by 2027) while exempting individual earned wage income in New Hampshire.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Grava dividendos e intereses (eliminándose para 2027) eximiendo sueldos y salarios individuales en New Hampshire.
