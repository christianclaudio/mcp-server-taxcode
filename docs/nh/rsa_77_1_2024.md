# N.H. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 77:1 — Rate of Tax on Interest and Dividends (Repealed Effective 2025)

- **Jurisdiction:** NH
- **Corpus:** RSA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** 2025
- **Source:** [https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/v/77/77-1.htm](https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/v/77/77-1.htm)
- **Hierarchy:** `RSA > Title V > Chapter 77 > § 77:1`
- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposed 3.0% tax on dividend and interest income for 2024, fully repealed for tax periods beginning January 1, 2025 under 2023 HB 2 in New Hampshire.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impuso 3.0% sobre dividendos e intereses en 2024, totalmente derogado a partir del 1 de enero de 2025 bajo la ley HB 2 de 2023 de New Hampshire.
