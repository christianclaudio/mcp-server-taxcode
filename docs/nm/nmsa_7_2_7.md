# N.M. Stat. Ann. § 7-2-7 — Individual Income Tax Rates

- **Jurisdiction:** NM
- **Corpus:** NMSA
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://nmonesource.com/nmos/nmsa/en/item/4340/index.do#!fragment/sec7-2-7](https://nmonesource.com/nmos/nmsa/en/item/4340/index.do#!fragment/sec7-2-7)
- **Hierarchy:** `NMSA > Chapter 7 > Article 2 > § 7-2-7`
- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates from 1.7% to top rate 5.9% in New Mexico.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto sobre la renta individual graduadas desde 1.7% hasta tasa máxima de 5.9% en Nuevo México.
