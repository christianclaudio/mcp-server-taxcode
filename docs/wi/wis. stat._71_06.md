# Wis. Stat. § 71.06 — Rates of Tax - Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** WI
- **Corpus:** Wis. Stat.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/71/iii/06](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/71/iii/06)

- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates: 3.50%, 4.40%, 5.30%, and top rate 7.65% in Wisconsin.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto individual sobre ingresos: 3.50%, 4.40%, 5.30% y tasa máxima de 7.65% en Wisconsin.
