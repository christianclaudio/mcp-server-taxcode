# Okla. Stat. tit. 68, § 2355 — Tax Rates - Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** OK
- **Corpus:** 68 O.S.
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/DeliverDocument.asp?CiteID=440316](https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/DeliverDocument.asp?CiteID=440316)

- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates from 0.25% to top rate 4.75% in Oklahoma.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto sobre ingresos individuales graduadas desde 0.25% hasta tasa máxima de 4.75% en Oklahoma.
