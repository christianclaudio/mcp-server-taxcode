# S.C. Code Ann. § 12-6-510 — Tax Rates for Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** SC
- **Corpus:** S.C. Code
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force
- **Source:** [https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t12c006.php](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t12c006.php)
- **Hierarchy:** `S.C. Code > Title 12 > Chapter 6 > Article 5 > § 12-6-510`
- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes individual income tax with 0% on initial income and a top marginal rate of 6.40% in South Carolina.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone impuesto individual sobre ingresos con 0% en tramo inicial y tasa marginal máxima del 6.40% en Carolina del Sur.
