# Mo. Rev. Stat. § 143.011 — Tax on Resident Individuals

- **Jurisdiction:** MO
- **Corpus:** RSMo
- **Effective Year:** 2024
- **Superseded Year:** Current / In Force

- **Cross References:** 

## Operative Statutory Language (English)

Imposes graduated individual income tax rates with a top marginal rate of 4.80% in Missouri under SB 3 and SB 5.

## Texto Estatutario Oficial (Español)

Impone tasas de impuesto individual graduadas con una tasa marginal máxima de 4.80% en Missouri bajo SB 3 y SB 5.
